Plumula — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This policy explains how the Plumula app (the “App”), published by Vambran Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. Plumula diagnoses houseplants: you photograph a plant, the App reads the photo with an AI model, and it returns a diagnosis, a care plan and a follow-up chat. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The App has no account or sign-in. Your plants, diagnoses, notes and chats are stored on your device.
  • The photos you scan, and the questions you ask, are sent to our server and to an AI provider so a diagnosis or reply can be produced. This is the core of what the App does.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
The one thing worth reading twice: a plant photo is a photo taken with your camera. It may incidentally contain your home, your room, people, or (for a photo you pick from your library) embedded location data. Only scan what you're comfortable sending. See “Photos and AI processing” below.

Information you provide

  • Plant photos. Photos you capture in the App, or pick from your photo library to scan.
  • Text you type or dictate. Follow-up questions in the plant chat, progress check-ins (“how's it going?”), plant-name corrections, and notes.
  • Settings. Language, theme, watering units, reminder preferences.

Except for reported content (see “When you report an AI response” below), all of this is stored locally on your device, in the App's own database and private file storage. We do not keep a copy of your plant library on our servers, and we have no way to browse it. It is removed when you delete the plant in the App, or when you uninstall the App.

Photos and AI processing

To diagnose a plant, the App sends the photo plus the measured facts around it (a light reading, and optionally coarse local weather — see below) to our proxy server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. Our server forwards the request to an AI provider to produce the diagnosis. Depending on the request, the provider is one of:

  • Google (Gemini API) — privacy policy
  • Groq (fast inference of open AI models) — privacy policy

The same path is used for the follow-up chat, the “how's it going?” progress updates, and translating a saved diagnosis into another language — in those cases the text of your message (and, for a follow-up, the plant's latest photo) is what gets sent.

We do not store your photos or messages on our server. They are relayed to the AI provider to produce your result and are not written to any database of ours. Each provider then handles the request under its own policy, linked above. Two things to know here: Plumula currently uses Google's Gemini API on its free tier, and under Google's terms content sent on that tier may be used by Google to improve its services and may be reviewed by human reviewers; Groq uses a request only to generate the reply. Because of this, only scan and send what you're comfortable sharing — see the note at the top of this page.

Information collected automatically

  • Install identifier (usage limits). To enforce the free-trial and subscription scan limits, the App sends a per-installation identifier with each scan — on Android the Android ID, on iOS the vendor identifier (identifierForVendor). Our server stores only that identifier, a period (e.g. the month), and a count of scans. It is not linked to your name, email or device contacts, it carries none of your plant data, and it is not used for advertising or tracking across apps.
  • Analytics. The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used (for example, which screens are opened) so we can improve it. This collects app-usage events, general device and app information, and an app-instance identifier. We use it in aggregate; it is not used to identify you personally. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports. The App uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to record crashes and errors so we can fix them. A report includes the error and its stack trace, the state of the device at the time (device model, OS version, memory, and app version) and a Crashlytics installation identifier. It contains none of your plant photos or chat content. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Reported content (only if you use it). If you report an AI response as inappropriate, the App sends us the reported text, your optional note, and your install identifier so we can review it and improve safety. Unlike your plant data, these reports are stored on our server. See “When you report an AI response” below.
  • Purchases. The optional Plumula+ and Plumula Pro+ subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. Those platforms handle the transaction; we never receive or store your payment card details. The App knows your current subscription level so it can unlock the right features and count scans.

The App contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads. We do not use your data for advertising, ad personalization, or to track you across other apps or services.

When you report an AI response

Generative-AI results can occasionally be wrong or inappropriate. The App lets you report an AI diagnosis or chat reply from within the App. When you do, we receive the flagged text, any note you add, and your install identifier (so repeated reports can be correlated), and we store them so we can review the output and improve safety. You can have these deleted at any time — see “Data retention and deletion”.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph your plant and measure the light it's getting. Required to scan.
  • Photo library — to pick an existing photo to scan, and to save a plant photo back to your library when you tap Save.
  • Location (approximate, optional) — used only to look up local weather so the diagnosis can account for it. We request coarse location and send the coordinates to Open-Meteo (terms & privacy) to fetch a forecast, then pass a short weather summary to the diagnosis. If a photo you pick already has location saved in it (EXIF), that location may be used for the same weather lookup. Decline location and everything else still works.
  • Microphone & speech recognition (optional) — to dictate a message in the plant chat instead of typing. Transcription is performed by the operating system (Apple's Speech framework on iOS, Google's speech service on Android) and is subject to that platform's policy. We do not keep the audio.
  • Notifications (optional) — to send watering reminders and to tell you a diagnosis is ready. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

How we use information

On-device data is used only to provide the App's features (your plant history, timelines, reminders). Photos and messages are used to produce the diagnosis or reply you asked for. The install identifier is used only to count scans against your plan and to prevent abuse. Analytics and crash reports are used in aggregate to understand usage and fix problems. Store data is used by Apple/Google to process purchases.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share your plant library with anyone. The only sharing is with the service providers named above — the AI providers that produce a diagnosis (Google Gemini and Groq), Cloudflare (which hosts our proxy), Open-Meteo (weather, if you allow location), Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), and Apple/Google (purchases) — strictly to provide their part of the App.

Data retention and deletion

Your plants, photos, diagnoses and chats live only on your device: delete a plant or a scan in the App, or uninstall the App, to remove them. Photos and messages sent for a diagnosis are not retained by us. The scan counter tied to your install identifier is retained so limits still work if you reinstall.

You can erase what we hold at any time from Settings → Delete my data in the App. This resets the analytics identifier and asks our server to remove the data tied to your install: any reported content is deleted, and the scan counter is replaced with an anonymous, one-way hashed record so it cannot simply be reset by reinstalling. You can also contact us at the address below to make the same request.

Children's privacy

Plumula is a general-audience plant-care app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because the App stores your plant data only on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the App and in your device settings. For anything held on our side (the scan counter, and any reported content), use Settings → Delete my data or contact us below.

Security

Your plant data remains on your device, protected by your device's own security (lock screen, OS sandboxing). All network requests use secure (HTTPS) connections, and our proxy is locked to the App. Cloudflare processes your device's IP address transiently to route requests and rate-limit abuse; we do not store it. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
